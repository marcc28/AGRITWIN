# 1. What Information Do We Collect?

## Personal Information You Disclose to Us

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you register for an account, configure your profile, use our Services, manage agricultural information, or contact us.

Depending on how you use the Services, this information may include:

- Name
- Email address
- Password credentials
- Language preferences
- Measurement unit preferences
- Notification preferences
- Account and profile settings
- Information about agricultural fields
- Crop information
- Irrigation information
- Sensor configuration
- Images uploaded to the Services
- Other information that you voluntarily provide

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information unless necessary for a specific purpose and permitted by applicable law.

## Agricultural and Operational Information

Because our Services are designed for agricultural management, users may provide information relating to their agricultural activities.

This may include:

- Field names and locations
- Field areas and boundaries
- Crop types and varieties
- Planting and harvesting dates
- Irrigation information
- Sensor readings
- Weather-related information
- Agricultural images
- Pest detection results
- Crop predictions
- Task and activity records
- Three-dimensional field models

Some of this information may be associated with a user account and may therefore constitute personal information depending on the circumstances.

## Information Automatically Collected

When you access or use the Services, certain technical information may be collected automatically.

This may include:

- IP address
- Browser type
- Device type
- Operating system
- Language preferences
- Time zone
- Referring URLs
- Device identifiers
- Date and time of access
- General usage information
- Error and diagnostic information

We use this information primarily to operate, maintain, secure, and improve the Services.

## Location Information

Some features of the Services may process geographic information related to agricultural fields, sensors, images, or other resources.

Location information may include:

- Field coordinates
- Field boundaries
- Sensor locations
- Image coordinates
- Other geographic information provided by the user or connected systems

We restrict access to location information according to applicable authorisation requirements.
